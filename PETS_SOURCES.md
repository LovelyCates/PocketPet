The exact commit, manifest, image and license URLs and all SHA-256 digests are pinned in `community-pets.json`. The catalog and data-only downloader are included in the portable release. Pokémon and Xin Chen's image collections remain upstream because their terms limit personal use or bulk redistribution. Shorekeeper and Yangyang are included with their source and license files; those files preserve their upstream notices. Their images are not claimed as PocketPet originals.

Upstream terms distinguish documentation/code from images. The Pokémon sprites and Xin Chen's fan sprites are supplied for personal, non-commercial fan use; Yangyang's original expressive elements use CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 with attribution; Shorekeeper's repository includes its MIT license and third-party character notice. Retain the source files and consult each role's SOURCE-LICENSE.md before using its artwork outside this personal installation.

Denia is a user-provided package included with the project owner's authorization, with its source notice in `pets/denia/SOURCE.md`. Cloud Cat and Fox are the project's original sample assets. No project-wide license is asserted over third-party character designs or artwork.
